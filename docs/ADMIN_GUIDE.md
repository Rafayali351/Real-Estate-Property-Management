# REAL ESTATE — Executive Staff Operations Guide

Welcome to the **REAL ESTATE Management Platform**. This handbook is designed for office managers, concierge staff, and brokers to manage the site, properties, client viewings, and communications without touching code or database servers.

---

## 1. Accessing the Admin Console

1. Navigate to the website homepage and click **Sign In** or go to `https://yourdomain.com/login`.
2. Enter your administrator email or phone and password.
3. You will be redirected to the **Verification Screen**. Choose whether you wish to receive your 6-digit code via **Email** or **SMS**.
4. Enter the 6-digit code. Because your account has administrator privileges, you will automatically be directed into the **Executive Cockpit** at `/admin`.
5. You will also see an **"Admin Management"** link inside your top avatar menu at any time.

---

## 2. Managing Luxury Properties

### Adding a New Estate Listing
1. In the sidebar, select **Properties**.
2. Click the gold **"+ Add New Estate"** button.
3. Fill in:
   - **Title**: e.g., *The Margalla Crest Presidential Estate*.
   - **Architectural Description**: Highlight craftsmanship, views, ceiling heights, security features, and materials.
   - **Price**: Total price in PKR (no commas needed, the system formats it automatically).
   - **Listing Type**: Choose *Sale* (for acquisition) or *Rent* (for lease).
   - **Address & City**: Location in Pakistan (Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi, Rawalpindi, Gwadar).
   - **Typology**: Mansion, Sky Penthouse, Villa, Modern Estate, or Apartment.
   - **Beds, Baths, Sq. Ft.**: Enter numbers.
   - **Cover Image**: Click to browse or drop an image (JPG, PNG, or WEBP under 5MB). The system automatically saves and optimizes the image.
   - **Assigned Advisor**: Select the advisor responsible for this listing.
   - **Featured Toggle**: Check this box if you want this property featured prominently on the homepage.
4. Click **Publish Estate**. It is immediately live.

### Editing or Soft-Deleting a Property
- Click the **Pencil icon** on any property row to update prices, photos, or descriptions.
- Click the **Trash icon** to soft-delete a property. Soft-deleted properties are immediately hidden from public searches and client views while preserving booking history.

## 3. Handling Private Tour Bookings

Clients schedule viewings for available estates through the website.
1. In the sidebar, select **Tour Bookings**.
2. New requests arrive marked as **"Pending"** (Cyan badge).
3. Click **Review & Action** on any booking:
   - View the client's name, phone, email, requested date/time, and private notes.
   - **Confirm Appointment**: Changes status to *Confirmed* and automatically emails the client an official luxury confirmation.
   - **Cancel Tour**: Changes status to *Cancelled* and emails the client with your reason.
   - **Mark Completed**: Use this after the physical viewing has taken place.
   - **Concierge Note**: Add a message (e.g. *"Gate pass 491 issued. Valet parking reserved."*). This note is included in the email sent to the client.

---

## 4. Managing Client Inquiries & Replying

1. In the sidebar, select **Client Inquiries**.
2. Messages sent through the website contact form or property pages appear here.
3. Click **Open & Reply**:
   - The system automatically marks the message as **Read**.
   - Read the client's inquiry.
   - Type your response into the reply box and click **Transmit Email Reply**.
   - The platform formats your response into a branded luxury HTML email and dispatches it straight to the client's inbox.
   - The inquiry is now stamped as **"Replied"** with your response archived.

---

## 5. Managing Advisors & Agents

1. In the sidebar, select **Advisors & Agents**.
2. Click **"+ Register New Advisor"**.
3. Enter their full name, official email, phone, specialization, and upload a portrait photograph.
4. Active advisors appear on the homepage and can be assigned to property listings.

---

## 6. Blocking Problematic Users

1. In the sidebar, select **Registered Users**.
2. Search by name, email, or telephone number.
3. Click **Block User** to immediately restrict an account. Blocked users cannot schedule tours or log into the portal.
4. Click **Unblock** to restore access at any time. (Administrators cannot block their own accounts).

---

## 7. Updating Company Contact & Site Settings

1. In the sidebar, select **Site Settings**.
2. Update company name, phone numbers, WhatsApp line, headquarters address, or social media links.
3. Click **Save Site Settings**. The public website navbar, footer, and email headers update immediately without any code edits.
